## Required reviewer questions for EACH final image

1. What is the full OCI manifest digest and image config ID, exact build commit,
   architecture and immutable SBOM archive SHA-256?
2. Which SPDX expressions/dual licences, vendored sources, copyright notices,
   modified binaries, generated fonts/icons and static Go modules are present?
3. Are EPL/MPL/LGPL/GPL/AGPL or network-copyleft obligations triggered by
   the **actual** linkage, modifications or redistribution mode?
4. Does the assembled customer-facing NOTICE include attribution, license
   texts, modifications, source availability and source-offer instructions?
5. Has Root.io or another external patch provider specifically authorized
   redistribution of **its** patched binaries and source/patch text?
6. Can the authorized, independent reviewer sign the precise immutable image
   and attached NOTICE/source-offer document, or document actionable blockers?
